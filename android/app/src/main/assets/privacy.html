Privacy Policy — VARUGAI Attendance Ledger

Android package: com.gasczoology.varugai
App version: 15.1.4
Effective date: 22 September 2026

VARUGAI Attendance Ledger is an offline-first application intended for teachers, departments and academic institutions to record, manage and review student attendance.

Information handled by the app

Depending on user configuration, VARUGAI may store student names, roll numbers or identifiers, course/class details, period-wise attendance, practical-session attendance, notes, and user-selected or captured photo evidence associated with attendance records.

Local storage and offline operation

Attendance and roster data are stored locally on the user's device. VARUGAI does not require continuous internet access for its core attendance functions and does not upload attendance records, student rosters, or stored photo evidence to developer-operated servers.

Files, backup and export

VARUGAI can create reports and backups when the user explicitly requests them. The Android system document picker is used to let the user choose the destination. The user is responsible for protecting exported copies.

Camera and photo evidence

When the user chooses a photo-based workflow, Android's system camera, photo picker or document picker may be invoked as appropriate. Selected files and images are used for the requested attendance or import function and are not uploaded to developer-operated servers by VARUGAI.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

VARUGAI does not require an online account for its core functions, does not sell personal information, and is not designed to display third-party advertising or send classroom attendance data to third-party behavioural analytics services.

Retention, deletion and institutional responsibility

Locally stored data remains until it is deleted through the app, Android app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled, subject to any exported backups retained separately. Teachers and institutions are responsible for lawful entry, access control, retention, consent and protection of student information under their applicable rules.

Security

VARUGAI minimises unnecessary exposure by keeping core attendance data on the device. Users should secure the device and any exported backups appropriately.
