Semester setup

MSU assesses eligibility course by course, so keep one register per course. Defaults follow the published bands: 75% required, 65–74% condonation on payment of the fee, 50–64% condonation with a medical certificate, below 50% repeat. Confirm them against your current regulation before relying on them.

Set the class and the date range, generate the calendar, then switch off holidays and set the hours actually taken on any short day.

Switch between classes with the selector at the top of the screen. Duplicate copies the roster and the calendar into a new register with no attendance — use it for a second course taught to the same class.

Working days — holidays, short days and special Saturdays

Every date is explicitly Working or Holiday. An excluded day contributes nothing to any student's denominator.

Roster

CSV, TSV, plain text, Excel (.xlsx) or Word (.docx). If the list exists digitally anywhere, use this — it cannot mistype a name.

One student per line as roll, name. Order is kept exactly as typed — register order, never alphabetical. A student admitted later goes at the bottom with a start date.

Semester grid

Every student, every hour of the semester. One tap per student per day. The cell cycles blank → present → absent → on duty. Use All P to fill a whole day, then tap only the absentees. Press Complete when the day is finished — that is what puts the day into the percentage.
